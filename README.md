## 💖 About

**Chibi-FX** is a lightweight 2D VTuber application designed to make streaming and video creation simple, fun, and accessible.

Whether you're a content creator, streamer, or hobbyist, Chibi-FX helps bring your animated character to life with minimal setup.

---

## ✨ Features

🎭 **Real-Time Avatar Animation**  
Animate your character while streaming or recording.

🎤 **Microphone-Based Lip Sync**  
Automatic mouth movement driven by your voice input.

🎚️ **Adjustable Sensitivity**  
Fine-tune reaction thresholds to match your microphone and speaking style.

🪶 **Lightweight & Easy to Use**  
Simple setup with a clean and focused interface.

🎥 **Content Creator Friendly**  
Designed for both live streaming and video production.

🟩 **Green Screen Support**  
Perfect for OBS and other recording software.

⚙️ **Built with Godot**  
Powered by the open-source Godot Engine.
